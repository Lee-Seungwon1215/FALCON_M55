FAEST. The results for FAEST are shown in Table 4. For standard FAEST, speedups
range from 1.10x to 1.23x for the fast variant and from 1.01x to 1.05x for the short
variant. The gains are more modest than those observed for AlMer because AIM2 is built
directly over GF(2ł), while FAEST uses AES (which operates in GF(28)) as its one-way
function and only relies on GF(2') arithmetic in the surrounding zero-knowledge proof.
For FAEST-EM, which reduces the number of AES evaluations and therefore increases
the relative contribution of field arithmetic to the total cost, the gains are more significant,
reaching 1.30x for 256-bit level signatures in the fast variant. FAEST key generation data
was omitted, as the specific GF (2°) finite field used for signature generation was not the
focus of this optimization work.
