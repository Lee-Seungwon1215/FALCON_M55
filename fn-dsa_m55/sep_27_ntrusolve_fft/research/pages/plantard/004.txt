2.2 NTRU and NTTRU
NTRU, as a finalist in the third round of the NIST PQC project, is the combination of
two first-round candidates, NTRUEncrypt [ZCHW] and NTRU-HRSS-KEM [SHRS]. The
KEM specified in the NTRU’s specification is constructed using a generic transformation
of a correct deterministic public-key encryption scheme (correct DPKE)[CDH+20]. The
polynomial arithmetic of NTRU operates in three polynomial rings Z3[x]/Φn,Zq[x]/Φn,
and Zq[x]/(Φ1·Φn) with Φ1 = (x−1) and Φn = xn−1 + xn−2 +···+ 1.

correct probabilistic public-key encryption scheme (partially correct PPKE). The speed
of NTTRU is faster than other NTRU variants, including the third-round finalist NTRU
