3.2 Proof of the Improved Plantard Multiplication
Theorem 1 states the correctness of Algorithm 10, and we will give the specific proof below.
Recently, Yang et al. [YJX24] provided an insightful derivation of the Montgomery type
algorithms, including the improved Plantard arithmetic. In their work, they identified
a misuse of the floor and ceiling functions in our approach. Their findings were further
supported by counterexamples that exposed flaws in the original algorithm. Upon careful
analysis, we realized that we had inaccurately applied the rounding property of the right-
shift operation during the initial proof. To rectify this issue, we have made the necessary
corrections in our proof. The main update we made in the algorithm is to ensure α>0
instead of α≥0 in the original paper. This update will not affect the correctness of the
Kyber implementation as we were using α= 3.
