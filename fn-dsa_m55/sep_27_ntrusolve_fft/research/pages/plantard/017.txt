4.2.4 Double Plantard Reduction for Packed Coefficients
The modular reduction of coefficients is necessary if the next operation in NTT/INTT
would cause an overflow. Previous work [ABCG20] uses double Montgomery reduction or
double Barrett reduction (i.e., Algorithm 10 and Algorithm 8 in [ABCG20]) to reduce two
packed coefficients on Cortex-M4, which consume 7 cycles and 8 cycles, respectively. The
double Barrett reduction in [ABCG20, Algorithm 8] requires two explicit shift operations
for packed coefficients. Recent work further reduces its cycle counts down to 6 cycles by
eliminating two explicit shift operations using the smlawb and smlawt instructions (see
[AHKS22, Algorithm 3.2]). In this work, an even better 5-cycle double Plantard reduction
is proposed and shown in Algorithm 15. This algorithm is based on the fact that the
Plantard reduction over a 16-bit signed integer can be viewed as a Plantard multiplication
by a constant−22lmod q. And one can multiply q−1 by the constant−22lmod q to obtain
a precomputed constant, thus saving one multiplication by q−1. Note that the input range
of Algorithm 15 is the same as Algorithm 11. Since Algorithm 15 generates signed output
