We then apply VSEB to renormalize this expansion into a shorter, well-formed
one. VSEB maintains a small accumulator ϵ and scans the list, repeatedly comput-
ing ri,ϵi+1 = 2Sum(ϵi,ei+1). If ϵi+1 ̸= 0, it emits ri as the next output component
and carries ϵi+1 forward, else it carries ri forward without emitting an output. This

puts equals the original total, while producing a compact, P-nonoverlapping expansion.
