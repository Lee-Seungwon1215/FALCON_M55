6.3.1 Integrating Toom-Cook, Karatsuba, and Schoolbook
We implement a degree 256 negacyclic polynomial through a combination of one layer
of 4-way Toom-Cook for degree 256 →64 reduction, one layer of Karatsuba for degree
32 →16 reduction, and an integrated 32 ↔16 followed by 16 ×16 schoolbook. We found
this variant slightly more eﬃcient than an implementation of degree-32 schoolbook.
