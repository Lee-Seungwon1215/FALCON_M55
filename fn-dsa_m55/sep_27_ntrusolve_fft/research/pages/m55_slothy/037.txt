8.4 Example: X25519 on AArch64
This section discusses the use of slothy for the optimization of the X25519 scalar
multiplication on the Cortex-A55 CPU. The complexity of this example significantly
exceeds that of the previous examples, both because of code size and because of the
breadth of different scalar and SIMD instructions used in it.
