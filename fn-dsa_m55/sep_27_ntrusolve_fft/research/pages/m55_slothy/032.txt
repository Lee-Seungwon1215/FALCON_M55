8.2.2 Readable base implementations
We provide readable base implementations for the complete Kyber and Dilithium NTTs.
Here, we comment on some selected aspects only.
Modular multiplication strategy. For the underlying modular multiplication, one has
the choice between “rounding Montgomery multiplication”, introduced in [BHK+21] and
used in [BBMK+21], or “Barrett multiplication”, also introduced in [BHK+21] and used
in [BHK+22]. We use Barrett multiplication throughout as it leads to smaller coefficient
growth than rounding Montgomery multiplication.
Initial layers. Listing 9 shows a naïve implementation of the first two layers of the
forward NTT for Dilithium, using macros to keep the implementation readable. While
readable, however, it performs very poorly: The back-to-back instances of load/store and
multiplication instructions mean that only little use is made of instruction overlapping.
