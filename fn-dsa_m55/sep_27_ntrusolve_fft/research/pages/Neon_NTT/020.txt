5 Result
We provide benchmarking results on the Arm Cortex-A72 processor and the Apple M1.
Arm Cortex-A72. The Arm Cortex-A72 CPU implements the Armv8-A architecture and
has a triple-issue out-of-order pipeline. Specifically, we use the Raspberry Pi 4 Model B
featuring the quad-core Broadcom BCM2711 chipset. It comes with a 32 kB L1 data cache,
a 48 kB L1 instruction cache, and a 1 MB L2 cache and runs at 1.5 GHz. For hashing, we
use the SHA-3/SHAKE Neon implementation by Nguyen et al. [NG21]. For benchmarking
individual functions we make use of the cycle counter of the PMU. For benchmarking the
full cryptographic schemes, we use SUPERCOP.5 We use gcc version 10.3.0 with -O3.
